## Part 0 — What SR-1 is

RM-1 gave the governed return series its risk reading — volatility and drawdown per trailing window. SR-1 is the **risk-adjusted return**: the Sharpe ratio, `SR = mean(excess) / σ(excess)`, per trailing window on the same calendar-month grid, where `excess_j = m_j − r_f,j` against a **captured risk-free return series**. It is the number an allocator quotes first and the one every prior slice deferred.

It deliberately reuses RM-1's machinery — the month relink, the alignment gate, the `stats_kernel` estimators, the window shape, the four-column grain pattern, the suppression encoding — and adds exactly two new things: the risk-free leg, and the ratio.

## Part 2 — Rule-6 external grounding (sources checked 2026-07-28)

Grades: **[V]** verified against a fetched primary source · **[C]** independently computed · **[U]** ours, never cited.

- **Sharpe (1966), "Mutual Fund Performance"** — the original "reward-to-variability" ratio: mean return **in excess of the riskless rate** over σ of the **total** return series (the draft misstated the numerator; the denominator contrast — σ(total) vs 1994's σ(differential) — is the load-bearing half and stands). **[V]**
- **Sharpe (1994), "The Sharpe Ratio"** — the revised, now-canonical form: the **differential return** `d_t = R_Ft − R_Bt` (fund minus benchmark, here the risk-free), `SR = mean(d)/σ(d)`. **The denominator is σ of the EXCESS series.** SR-1 v1 implements Sharpe-1994; the 1966 σ(portfolio) form is exactly the "reuse RM-1's volatility" shortcut, and it is refused as a *silent* choice — if ever wanted, it is a second declared metric, not a substitution. **[V]**
- **Lo (2002), "The Statistics of Sharpe Ratios"** — under **iid** returns `SR(q) = √q · SR(1)` (the ×√12 monthly→annual operator); under autocorrelation this misstates, with the correction in Eq. 20 (exact only on log returns). SR-1 carries **√12 as the DECLARED iid convention** — the same demotion honesty as RM-1's Lo usage, and consistent with RM-1 carrying k=12 as its only grounded basis. The autocorrelation-corrected annualizer is a recorded v2 (this platform's own desmoothing slices exist because iid is false for its books — stated, not hidden). **[V]**
- **GIPS 2020** — does NOT require or define a Sharpe ratio. If presented, it is an "additional risk measure": **4.C.43.a** (describe it) and **4.C.44** (gross/net disclosure) apply; rows are gross-of-fees inherited from PM-1 and say so. **[V]**

## Part 3 — Design decisions

### OD-SR-1-A — The construction (Tier-3), with every operand DECLARED (verifier B1/B2/B3 folds)

Per trailing window `W ∈ {12, 36}` ending at each month-end, over the relinked monthly series `m_j` and the captured risk-free series `r_f,j`:

- `excess_j = m_j − r_f,j` — both 12dp fractions on the same month grid; the subtraction is exact at 12dp (verifier-executed, incl. sign/magnitude extremes).
- **`SHARPE_RATIO`: single-quantization, and the order is DECLARED (B1).** The mean and σ of the excess series are accumulated **UNQUANTIZED at 50-digit context** (the `stats_kernel` internals), the division performed at that precision, and the result quantized ONCE to 12dp. This is a deliberate **contrast** with RM-1's stored-value order — RM-1 persists its σ, so its ×√12 must read the stored value to make the pair reconcile; **ENT-065 persists no separate mean or σ**, so no reconciliation constraint binds SR's internals and single-quantization is strictly more accurate. The alternative (dividing the quantized values) was executed and refuted: a NON-constant series can quantize to σ = `0E-12` and raise `DivisionByZero` — an uncaught 500 on a legal input.
- **The suppression predicate names the SAME σ (B1):** suppressed iff the **unquantized** σ is exactly zero — i.e. a genuinely constant excess series (Decimal equality is exact). Sub-quantum dispersion divides finely and emits a (large) value, which the magnitude gate below then adjudicates. The suppression reason says "zero dispersion — the ratio is undefined for a constant excess series" and is TRUE under this predicate.
- **`SHARPE_RATIO_ANN` = `quantize(SR_stored × √12)`** — from the STORED 12dp SR (this pair IS persisted, so RM-1's reconciliation order applies to it), basis `SQRT_12`, the iid caveat in doc + registered assumptions. Grounded by Lo (2002) `SR(q)=√q·SR(1)` AND by Sharpe (1994) eqs. 7/8 themselves (mean ×T, σ ×√T under zero serial correlation — the construction's own source carries its annualizer; verifier m3).
- **THE MAGNITUDE GATE, present from birth (B2).** The ratio is UNBOUNDED on admitted inputs — verifier-executed: twelve column-legal months yield SR = 10¹⁰, past both `Numeric(20,12)` and the house `1E7` envelope. The gate applies to the **EMITTED value, per row, covering the annualized member of the pair** (a column-fitting SR of 9×10⁶ still overflows at ×√12): breach ⇒ a **committed FAILED run + DQ evidence, zero rows** — the P3-8/RM-1 convention verbatim, with its executed control owed in the test list.
- **The divisor DIVERGES from the named paper, and the divergence is DISCLOSED, not branded away (B3).** Sharpe (1994)'s own endnote 1 uses the **population** standard deviation (divisor T); SR-1 uses `sample_stdev` (n−1), making σ larger by √(12/11) ≈ +4.4% at n=12 and SR correspondingly **smaller ~4.3%** — above this record's own materiality bar. The construction is therefore described everywhere (including the REGISTERED assumptions) as: *"Sharpe (1994)'s differential-return form with OUR n−1 divisor — a DISCLOSED divergence from the paper's population-σ endnote, following the platform's uniform `stats_kernel` estimator and GIPS practice."* The fidelity grade on OQ-SR-1-1 is corrected accordingly.
- **Metric-row inventory, ENUMERATED (M4):** every computable window emits BOTH `SHARPE_RATIO` (basis `NONE`) and `SHARPE_RATIO_ANN` (basis `SQRT_12`) — **including W=12**, because `√12·SR ≠ SR` at every window; RM-1's W=12 redundancy suppression rationale (exponent exactly 1) does NOT transfer and is not imported. Unfillable windows suppress BOTH metrics; the zero-dispersion case suppresses BOTH.

**Preconditions, binder-side (the kernel raises stay defense-in-depth):**

- the five-condition month alignment on the portfolio leg (reused);
- **`1+m>0` — kept as a POLICY precondition, with the honest argument (M1).** The draft's justification ("every RM-1 argument applies") was refuted by execution: Sharpe's arithmetic computes cleanly at −100% and −150% (no wealth index, no geometric exponent). The real grounds: the monthly series is SHARED SUBSTRATE — a book RM-1 refuses to carry a drawdown for must not quietly carry a Sharpe, and a ≤−100% month means the PM-1 series itself is degenerate (the no-cash-ledger pathology). Declared as policy, not domain necessity.
- **risk-free completeness — exactly ONE current-head rf return per MEASURED MONTH of the span (M2, both lanes).** Measured months = the months contributing a monthly observation (18 for the stage-16 book; d₀'s month needs NO rf row). A missing month is a **pre-create REFUSAL naming the month** — deliberately ASYMMETRIC with the per-window suppression convention, and the asymmetry is the point: window-insufficiency is structural and time fills it; a missing rf month is a CAPTURE GAP the operator must fix, and computing "some windows" over a gappy rf series ships a partially-poisoned surface. NO imputation, no carry-forward.
- **the rf month-JOIN, declared (M3, both lanes — the load-bearing NEW criterion the draft omitted):** rf rows join the measured month by **month-key of `return_date`** (`(year, month)`), so a last-weekday book against a calendar-month-end vendor aligns without either side bending dates; an rf row may be dated ANY day of its month. **More than one current-head rf row in one measured month is a pre-create refusal** — enforced by the BINDER (the draft's "the current-head read makes this structural" was FALSE: the grain keys on `return_date`, so two dates in one month are both current heads; the claim is withdrawn and the binder assert is the control). The builder pins rf rows dated within [first measured month's start, span end]; a pinned row whose month is not a measured month is a refusal (an unconsumed pin is a lie in the provenance).
- **uniform series** — one `(benchmark_id, return_type=SIMPLE, return_basis)` per run (mixed refused), the basis **echoed on every row** (M6, the ENT-054 precedent) as `rf_return_basis`.
- A negative or zero `r_f` is legal input; **r_f ≤ −100% is STRUCTURALLY unreachable** — ENT-052's capture-side DQ RANGE is `> −1` strict (`benchmark_series.py:190-191`, whole-unit rollback), cited per this record's own structural-claims standard (verifier m2).
